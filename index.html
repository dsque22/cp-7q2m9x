Start here

Liao is a thick herbal syrup you mix into warm water and drink before meals.

It is for people whose stomach makes them pay for eating. Bloating and belching after almost every meal. Food sitting there for hours like it has not moved. The burn that shows up an hour later. Acid coming back up at night.

What you are actually selling

Life is meant to enjoy. We help people find the balance to do it.

That means ordering what everyone else is ordering. Eating the pasta, the bread and the wine, and then just getting on with your evening. Selective, never restrictive.

Call it Liao

On camera, always say Liao. Never say Reflux Relief, even if that is what is printed on the bottle you were sent. The name is changing and we do not want the old one in your video.

The one shot we need in almost every video
1Pour one spoon into a cup of warm water.
2Stir it. Show how thick it is.
3Drink it 30 minutes before you eat. Twice a day.

Film the pour and the stir close up. It is the single most useful thing you can give us, and it is what makes a video look like a real routine instead of an ad.
